This document records the source and editorial decisions for
[AN 8.6: Dutiyalokadhamma Sutta](an8-6-dutiyalokadhamma-sutta.md).

## Governing Decisions

- `aṭṭha lokadhammā` is `eight worldly conditions`. The compact list is `gain
  and loss, fame and disrepute, blame and praise, pleasure and pain`.
- `anuparivattanti` is `turn around`: the conditions move through the world and
  people in the world keep moving around them.
- `cittaṁ pariyādāya tiṭṭhati` is `takes over their heart`. The phrase describes
  domination, not the mere occurrence of a pleasant or painful event.
- `anurujjhati` / `paṭivirujjhati` is `welcomes` / `resists`. This keeps the
  paired response active and audible in both halves.
- `anicco dukkho vipariṇāmadhammo` is `impermanent, dissatisfying, and bound to
  change`. `Dissatisfying` states the quality of the condition rather than
  claiming every instance is felt as pain.
- `sukha` / `dukkha` in the eight-member list is the experiential pair
  `pleasure` / `pain`; the final doctrinal `dukkha` remains `dissatisfaction`.
- Every abbreviated condition is expanded. The full parallel is the practical
  exercise: notice the event, see its instability, and do not let it take over.
