## Translation

### The Setting

So I heard.

At one time the Buddha was staying near Sāvatthī, in Jeta's Grove,
Anāthapiṇḍika's monastery. There the Buddha addressed the bhikkhus: "Bhikkhus."

"Bhadante," the bhikkhus replied. The Buddha said this:

### The Eight Factors

"Bhikkhus, when the observance day is kept with its eight factors, it bears
great fruit and great benefit; it is glorious and far-reaching. And how is the
observance day kept with its eight factors so that it bears great fruit and
great benefit, and is glorious and far-reaching?

Bhikkhus, a noble disciple reflects: 'For as long as they live, the arahants
give up killing living beings and refrain from it. They have set aside rod and
sword. They are scrupulous and kind, and live full of sympathy for all living
beings.

'Today, for this night and day, I give up killing living beings and refrain
from it. I set aside rod and sword. I am scrupulous and kind, and I live full
of sympathy for all living beings.

'In this factor I follow the example of the arahants, and I will have kept the
observance day.'

This is the first factor they have.

'For as long as they live, the arahants give up taking what is not given and
refrain from it. They take only what is given and expect only what is given,
and they live cleanly, without stealing.

'Today, for this night and day, I give up taking what is not given and refrain
from it. I take only what is given and expect only what is given, and I live
cleanly, without stealing.

'In this factor I follow the example of the arahants, and I will have kept the
observance day.'

This is the second factor they have.

'For as long as they live, the arahants give up unchaste conduct. They live the
celibate life, set apart, refraining from sex, the villager's way.

'Today, for this night and day, I give up unchaste conduct. I live the celibate
life, set apart, refraining from sex, the villager's way.

'In this factor I follow the example of the arahants, and I will have kept the
observance day.'

This is the third factor they have.

'For as long as they live, the arahants give up false speech and refrain from
it. They speak the truth and hold to the truth; they are reliable and
dependable, and do not deceive the world.

'Today, for this night and day, I give up false speech and refrain from it. I
speak the truth and hold to the truth; I am reliable and dependable, and do not
deceive the world.

'In this factor I follow the example of the arahants, and I will have kept the
observance day.'

This is the fourth factor they have.

'For as long as they live, the arahants give up liquor, wine, and intoxicants,
which are a basis for heedlessness, and refrain from them.

'Today, for this night and day, I give up liquor, wine, and intoxicants, which
are a basis for heedlessness, and refrain from them.

'In this factor I follow the example of the arahants, and I will have kept the
observance day.'

This is the fifth factor they have.

'For as long as they live, the arahants eat once a day, refraining from eating
at night and at the wrong time.

'Today, for this night and day, I eat once a day, refraining from eating at
night and at the wrong time.

'In this factor I follow the example of the arahants, and I will have kept the
observance day.'

This is the sixth factor they have.

'For as long as they live, the arahants give up dancing, singing, music, and
shows, and wearing garlands, scents, and makeup, and adorning and beautifying
themselves, and refrain from them.

'Today, for this night and day, I give up dancing, singing, music, and shows,
and wearing garlands, scents, and makeup, and adorning and beautifying myself,
and refrain from them.

'In this factor I follow the example of the arahants, and I will have kept the
observance day.'

This is the seventh factor they have.

'For as long as they live, the arahants give up high and luxurious beds and
refrain from them. They sleep in a low place, on a small bed or a mat of grass.

'Today, for this night and day, I give up high and luxurious beds and refrain
from them. I sleep in a low place, on a small bed or a mat of grass.

'In this factor I follow the example of the arahants, and I will have kept the
observance day.'

This is the eighth factor they have.

When the observance day is kept with its eight factors like this, bhikkhus, it
bears great fruit and great benefit; it is glorious and far-reaching."

The first discourse is finished.
