# AN 2.9 Translation Notes

This document records the source and editorial decisions for
[AN 2.9: Cariya Sutta](an2-9-cariya-sutta.md).

## Source Base

- Primary source: SuttaCentral Bilara Mahāsaṅgīti root text, segment IDs
  `an2.9:1.1`–`an2.9:1.7`, stored in the bundled
  `.bilara-cache/an2.1-10_root-pli-ms.json` file.
- Canonical reader source: <https://suttacentral.net/an2.9/pli/ms>.
- The six substantive Pali segments contain 63 words. The prior roadmap's
  843-word figure counted the entire AN 2.1–10 bundle rather than AN 2.9.
- The Mahāsaṅgīti root title is `Cariyasutta`. Some English editions call the
  text `Lokapāla Sutta`, "Guardians of the World," after its opening formula.

## Governing Decisions

- `hiri` is `conscience`: the inward ethical response that recoils from an
  action because it would violate one's integrity. It is not guilt or toxic
  shame.
- `ottappa` is `moral caution`: alertness to the harm and consequences of an
  unwholesome action. It is not ordinary fear.
- The pair `hirī ca ottappañca` is therefore `conscience and moral caution`,
  matching the two governed major records and the revised compound record.
- `dve dhammā sukkā lokaṁ pālenti` is `two bright qualities protect the
  world`. `Bright` preserves the text's contrast with dark qualities in the
  preceding discourse; `protect` keeps the active social function.
- `nayidha paññāyetha` literally says that the listed relationships would no
  longer be recognized or distinguished. `People would stop respecting even
  the most basic sexual boundaries` states what the surrounding family list
  and animal comparison are doing rather than leaving a newcomer to infer it.
- `sambhedaṁ loko agamissa` is `the world would lose all sexual boundaries`.
  The line is not a general claim about disorder: the examples before and
  after it specifically concern sexual relationships.
- The ancient list is male-addressed and names a mother, mother's sister,
  uncle's wife, teacher's wife, and the wife of a respected person. The
  translation keeps that historical shape. The reader introduction explains
  the broader ethical point without silently rewriting the list.
