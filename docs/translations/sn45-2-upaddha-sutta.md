# SN 45.2: Upaḍḍha Sutta

  `good friendship, good companionship, and good fellowship`.

## Translation

### Ānanda's Claim

So I heard. At one time the Buddha was staying among the Sakyans, near a Sakyan
town called Nagaraka.

Then Venerable Ānanda went to the Buddha, bowed, sat to one side, and said:

"Bhante, good friendship, good companionship, and good fellowship are half of
the spiritual life."

### The Whole Spiritual Life

"Don't say that, Ānanda. Don't say that.

Good friendship, good companionship, and good fellowship are the whole of the
spiritual life.

You can expect a bhikkhu who has good friends, companions, and fellows to
develop the noble eightfold path and to keep developing it.

And how do they do that?

They develop right view supported by seclusion, fading, and quenching, maturing
in relinquishment.

They develop right intention supported by seclusion, fading, and quenching,
maturing in relinquishment.

They develop right speech supported by seclusion, fading, and quenching,
maturing in relinquishment.

They develop right action supported by seclusion, fading, and quenching,
maturing in relinquishment.

They develop right livelihood supported by seclusion, fading, and quenching,
maturing in relinquishment.

They develop right effort supported by seclusion, fading, and quenching,
maturing in relinquishment.

They develop right remembering supported by seclusion, fading, and quenching,
maturing in relinquishment.

They develop right composure supported by seclusion, fading, and quenching,
maturing in relinquishment.

That is how a bhikkhu with good friends, companions, and fellows develops the
noble eightfold path and keeps developing it.

### The Buddha as a Good Friend

Here is another way to understand why good friendship, good companionship, and
good fellowship are the whole of the spiritual life.

By relying on me as a good friend, beings subject to birth are freed from
birth. Beings subject to aging are freed from aging. Beings subject to death
are freed from death. Beings subject to sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness, and
distress are freed from sorrow, lamentation, pain, sadness, and distress.

This is another way to understand why good friendship, good companionship, and
good fellowship are the whole of the spiritual life."

The second discourse is finished.
