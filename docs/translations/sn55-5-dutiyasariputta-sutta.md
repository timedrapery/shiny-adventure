# SN 55.5: Dutiyasāriputta Sutta (The Stream and the Stream-Enterer)

## Editorial Note

- This is a repo-native study translation of SN 55.5, designed to be readable
  as a single Markdown document.
- Companion translator notes:
  [SN 55.5 Translation Notes](sn55-5-dutiyasariputta-sutta-notes.md)
- Source basis: the Pali text of SN 55.5 as segmented in SuttaCentral's Bilara
  data.
- House choices that most affect this sutta: `sotāpatti` is rendered
  `stream-entry` and `sotāpanna` `stream-enterer`; `sappurisa` is `good
  person`; `yoniso manasikāra` is `wise attention`; `ariyo aṭṭhaṅgiko maggo` is
  `the noble eightfold path`, with the eight factors in their governed
  renderings; and the `…ti vuccati` frame follows the `they speak of ...`
  wording already governed in
  [SN 12.15](sn12-15-kaccanagotta-sutta.md).
- Beware a homonym: the lexicon governs `sota` as `ear`, the hearing faculty.
  The `soto` of this discourse is the unrelated `stream` of stream-entry, and
  the governed sense-field rendering must not be applied to it.
- The source abridges the second recitation of the eight path factors with
  `…pe…`. This edition expands it, following the same call recorded for
  SN 12.11 and in the MN 64 notes.
- This surface follows [../PLAIN_ENGLISH_STANDARD.md](../PLAIN_ENGLISH_STANDARD.md).
- This edition treats SN 55.5 as the repository's control surface for the four
  factors of stream-entry and for the definition of the stream as the path.

## Translation

### The Setting

Then Venerable Sāriputta went to the Buddha, bowed, and sat down to one side.
The Buddha said to him:

### The Factors of Stream-Entry

"Sāriputta, they speak of 'a factor of stream-entry, a factor of
stream-entry.' What is a factor of stream-entry?"

"Bhante, keeping company with good persons is a factor of stream-entry.
Hearing the true Dhamma is a factor of stream-entry. Wise attention is a factor
of stream-entry. Practicing in line with the Dhamma is a factor of
stream-entry."

"Good, good, Sāriputta. Keeping company with good persons is a factor of
stream-entry. Hearing the true Dhamma is a factor of stream-entry. Wise
attention is a factor of stream-entry. Practicing in line with the Dhamma is a
factor of stream-entry.

### The Stream

They speak of 'the stream, the stream.' What is the stream?"

"Bhante, this noble eightfold path is itself the stream: right view, right
attitude, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right effort, right
remembering, right composure."

"Good, good, Sāriputta. This noble eightfold path is itself the stream: right
view, right attitude, right speech, right action, right livelihood, right
effort, right remembering, right composure.

### The Stream-Enterer

They speak of 'a stream-enterer, a stream-enterer.' What is a stream-enterer?"

"Bhante, anyone who has this noble eightfold path is called a stream-enterer —
this venerable one, with such a name, from such a family."

"Good, good, Sāriputta. Anyone who has this noble eightfold path is called a
stream-enterer — this venerable one, with such a name, from such a family."

The fifth discourse is finished.
