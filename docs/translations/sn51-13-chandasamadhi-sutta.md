# SN 51.13: Chandasamādhi Sutta (Unification Through Desire)

## Editorial Note

- This is a repo-native study translation of SN 51.13, designed to be readable
  as a single Markdown document.
- Companion translator notes:
  [SN 51.13 Translation Notes](sn51-13-chandasamadhi-sutta-notes.md)
- Source basis: the Pali text of SN 51.13 as segmented in SuttaCentral's
  Bilara data.
- House choices that most affect this sutta: `chanda` is rendered `desire`;
  `iddhipāda` is rendered `basis of power`; `vīmaṁsā` is rendered
  `investigation`; `vīriya` is rendered `energy`; `citta` is rendered `heart`;
  `samādhi` is rendered `unification of mind`; `ekaggatā` is rendered
  `directness`; `padhāna` is rendered `exertion`, so `padhānasaṅkhārā` are
  `exertion conditioners`; and `kusala` and `akusala` are rendered
  `wholesome` and `unwholesome`.
- This edition treats SN 51.13 as the control passage for the four bases of
  power, and for `chanda` in a wholesome role rather than beside the
  sensuality family.
- The discourse is built from one formula repeated four times. The Pali writes
  the first round in full and abbreviates the other three; this edition marks
  the elisions rather than silently expanding them.
- This is the first stable study edition of the repository's SN 51.13
  translation surface.

## Translation

### Unification Through Desire

"Bhikkhus, if a bhikkhu gains unification of mind in dependence on desire, gains
directness of heart, this is called unification through desire.

They generate desire for the non-arising of unarisen harmful, unwholesome
qualities; they make an effort, arouse energy, take hold of the heart,
and exert themselves.

They generate desire for the abandoning of arisen harmful, unwholesome
qualities; they make an effort, arouse energy, take hold of the heart,
and exert themselves.

They generate desire for the arising of unarisen wholesome qualities; they
make an effort, arouse energy, take hold of the heart, and exert
themselves.

They generate desire for the continuation, non-confusion, increase,
abundance, development, and fulfilment of arisen wholesome qualities; they
make an effort, arouse energy, take hold of the heart, and exert
themselves.

These are called the exertion conditioners.

So there is this desire, and this unification through desire, and these exertion
conditioners.

This, bhikkhus, is called the basis of power that possesses unification through
desire together with the exertion conditioners.

### Unification Through Energy

Bhikkhus, if a bhikkhu gains unification of mind in dependence on energy, gains
directness of heart, this is called unification through energy.

They generate desire for the non-arising of unarisen ... they generate desire
for the continuation, non-confusion, increase, abundance, development, and
fulfilment of arisen wholesome qualities; they make an effort, arouse energy,
take hold of the heart, and exert themselves.

These are called the exertion conditioners.

So there is this energy, and this unification through energy, and these exertion
conditioners.

This, bhikkhus, is called the basis of power that possesses unification through
energy together with the exertion conditioners.

### Unification Through the Heart

Bhikkhus, if a bhikkhu gains unification of mind in dependence on the heart,
gains directness of heart, this is called unification through the
heart.

They generate desire for the non-arising of unarisen harmful ... they generate
desire for the continuation, non-confusion, increase, abundance, development,
and fulfilment of arisen wholesome qualities; they make an effort, arouse
energy, take hold of the heart, and exert themselves.

These are called the exertion conditioners.

So there is this heart, and this unification through the heart,
and these exertion conditioners.

This, bhikkhus, is called the basis of power that possesses unification through
the heart together with the exertion conditioners.

### Unification Through Investigation

Bhikkhus, if a bhikkhu gains unification of mind in dependence on investigation,
gains directness of heart, this is called unification through
investigation.

They generate desire for the non-arising of unarisen harmful, unwholesome
qualities; they make an effort, arouse energy, take hold of the heart,
and exert themselves ... they generate desire for the continuation,
non-confusion, increase, abundance, development, and fulfilment of arisen
wholesome qualities; they make an effort, arouse energy, take hold of the
heart, and exert themselves.

These are called the exertion conditioners.

So there is this investigation, and this unification through investigation, and
these exertion conditioners.

This, bhikkhus, is called the basis of power that possesses unification through
investigation together with the exertion conditioners."
