# AN 8.41 Translation Notes

This document is the companion rationale for
[AN 8.41: Saṅkhittūposatha Sutta](an8-41-sankhittuposatha-sutta.md). The main
translation is the primary study surface; this file records debated
translation choices, source-audit calls, and edition notes that govern it.

## Source Base

- Primary source: the Pali text of AN 8.41 as segmented in SuttaCentral's
  Bilara data (`an8.41:1.1` to `an8.41:10.2`), fetched from the upstream
  Bilara root and matching its hash in `reviews/source-pins.json`.
- Control surfaces: DN 2 for the first four precepts (killing, stealing,
  unchaste conduct, false speech) and for the high and luxurious beds; MN 118
  for `mahapphalo hoti mahānisaṁso`; Dhp 21-32 for `pamāda`; MN 11 and
  MN 117 for the setting.
- Working method: each of the eight factors has the same four-part frame:
  what the arahants do for life, what the disciple will do for this night
  and day, the line about following the arahants, and the count. The source
  writes all eight out, and so does the translation.

## Governing Decisions

- `uposatha` is `the observance day`, its record. `uposatho upavuttho` is
  `the observance day is kept`.
- `aṭṭhaṅgasamannāgata` is `with its eight factors`; `aṅga` is `factor`.
- `mahapphalo hoti mahānisaṁso mahājutiko mahāvipphāro` is `it bears great
  fruit and great benefit; it is glorious and far-reaching`. The first pair
  follows MN 118.
- `imināpaṅgena arahataṁ anukaromi` is `in this factor I follow the example
  of the arahants`.
- The killing factor follows DN 2: `set aside rod and sword`, `scrupulous and
  kind`, `full of sympathy for all living beings` (`anukampā` is `sympathy`).
- The stealing factor follows DN 2: `take only what is given and expect only
  what is given, and live cleanly, without stealing`.
- `abrahmacariya` is `unchaste conduct`, as in DN 2, and `methunā
  gāmadhammā` is `sex, the villager's way`. This is the one factor that is
  stricter than the five precepts: for the observance day, the lay disciple
  is celibate.
- The false-speech factor follows DN 2: `speak the truth and hold to the
  truth; reliable and dependable, and do not deceive the world`.
- `surāmerayamajjapamādaṭṭhāna` is `liquor, wine, and intoxicants, which are
  a basis for heedlessness`. `pamāda` is `heedlessness`, as in Dhp 21-32.
- `ekabhattikā rattūparatā viratā vikālabhojanā` is `eat once a day,
  refraining from eating at night and at the wrong time`.
- The seventh factor joins two items: shows (`naccagītavāditavisūkadassana`)
  and adornment (`mālāgandhavilepanadhāraṇamaṇḍanavibhūsanaṭṭhāna`). The
  translation keeps them as one factor, as the source does.
- `uccāsayanamahāsayana` is `high and luxurious beds`, as in DN 2;
  `nīcaseyyaṁ kappenti — mañcake vā tiṇasanthārake vā` is `sleep in a low
  place, on a small bed or a mat of grass`.

## Re-audit Calls

- `Paṭhamaṁ` is rendered `The first discourse is finished.`
- `arahant` is left untranslated, its record default.

## Practice Clarifications

- The observance day is not a vow for life. The disciple takes each factor
  `for this night and day`, and models it on the arahants, who live that way
  for life.
- The eight factors are the five precepts with celibacy in place of
  sexual restraint, plus three: one meal, no shows or adornment, and no
  luxurious beds.
