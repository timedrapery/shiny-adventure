## Translation

### The Setting

At one time the Buddha was staying among the Koliyans, at a Koliyan town
named Kakkarapatta. Then Dīghajāṇu the Koliyan went to the Buddha, bowed, and
sat down to one side. Seated there, he said to the Buddha:

"Bhante, we are householders who enjoy sensual pleasures. We live at home
crowded with children. We enjoy sandalwood from Kāsi, we wear garlands,
scents, and makeup, and we accept gold and silver. Bhante, please teach us
the Dhamma in a way that will lead to our welfare and happiness in this life,
and to our welfare and happiness in the life to come."

### Four Things for Welfare in This Life

"Byagghapajja, four things lead to the welfare and happiness in this life of a
son of good family. What four? Accomplishment in initiative, accomplishment in
protection, good friendship, and balanced living.

And what is accomplishment in initiative? Here, Byagghapajja, whatever work a
son of good family earns his living by, whether farming, trade, raising
cattle, archery, government service, or some other craft, he is skilled and
tireless in it. He has the judgment to see how to go about it, and he is able
to do it and to organize it. This is called accomplishment in initiative.

And what is accomplishment in protection? Byagghapajja, a son of good family
has wealth gained by initiative and effort, built up by the strength of his
arms, earned by the sweat of his brow, lawful and lawfully gained. He keeps it
guarded and protected, thinking: 'How can I keep rulers and thieves from taking
this wealth, fire from burning it, floods from sweeping it away, and unloved
heirs from taking it?' This is called accomplishment in protection.

And what is good friendship? Here, Byagghapajja, in whatever village or town
a son of good family lives, he spends time with the householders or their
children there, young or old, who are mature in conduct and accomplished in
confidence, conduct, generosity, and discernment. He talks and discusses
things with them. He learns from the confidence of those accomplished in
confidence, from the conduct of those accomplished in conduct, from the
generosity of those accomplished in generosity, and from the discernment of
those accomplished in discernment. This is called good friendship.

And what is balanced living? Byagghapajja, a son of good family knows what
comes in and what goes out, and lives in balance, neither extravagantly nor too
frugally, thinking: 'In this way what comes in will exceed what goes out, and
what goes out will not exceed what comes in.' Just as someone who weighs
things, or their apprentice, holds up a scale and knows, 'It has dipped by this
much, or risen by this much,' in the same way a son of good family knows what
comes in and what goes out, and lives in balance, neither extravagantly nor too
frugally, thinking: 'In this way what comes in will exceed what goes out, and
what goes out will not exceed what comes in.'

If a son of good family with a small income lives lavishly, people will say:
'This son of good family eats up his wealth like someone eating figs.' If a
son of good family with a large income lives meanly, people will say: 'This
son of good family will die like a starving man.' But when a son of good
family knows what comes in and what goes out, and lives in balance, neither
extravagantly nor too frugally, thinking: 'In this way what comes in will
exceed what goes out, and what goes out will not exceed what comes in,' this
is called balanced living.

### Four Drains and Four Inlets

Wealth gathered in this way has four drains: chasing women, drinking, gambling,
and having bad friends, bad companions, and bad associates. Suppose there were
a great reservoir with four inlets and four drains. If someone closed the
inlets and opened the drains, and the rain did not fall properly, the
reservoir could be expected to shrink, not grow. In the same way, wealth
gathered in this way has four drains: chasing women, drinking, gambling, and
having bad friends, bad companions, and bad associates.

Wealth gathered in this way has four inlets: not chasing women, not drinking,
not gambling, and having good friends, good companions, and good associates.
Suppose there were a great reservoir with four inlets and four drains. If
someone opened the inlets and closed the drains, and the rain fell properly,
the reservoir could be expected to grow, not shrink. In the same way, wealth
gathered in this way has four inlets: not chasing women, not drinking, not
gambling, and having good friends, good companions, and good associates.

Byagghapajja, these are the four things that lead to the welfare and happiness
in this life of a son of good family.

### Four Things for Welfare in the Life to Come

Byagghapajja, four things lead to the welfare and happiness in the life to come
of a son of good family. What four? Accomplishment in confidence,
accomplishment in conduct, accomplishment in generosity, and accomplishment in
discernment.

And what is accomplishment in confidence? Byagghapajja, a son of good family
has confidence. He places confidence in the awakening of the Tathāgata: 'So
indeed is the Buddha — an arahant, perfectly and fully awakened, accomplished
in knowledge and conduct, well-departed, knower of the world, unsurpassed guide
for those to be tamed, teacher of gods and humans, awakened, blessed.' This is
called accomplishment in confidence.

And what is accomplishment in conduct? Byagghapajja, a son of good family
refrains from taking life, from taking what is not given, from sexual
misconduct, from false speech, and from intoxicants that lead to heedlessness.
This is called accomplishment in conduct.

And what is accomplishment in generosity? Byagghapajja, a son of good family
lives at home with a heart free from the stain of stinginess. He is openhanded,
glad to let go, responsive when asked, and glad to give and share. This is
called accomplishment in generosity.

And what is accomplishment in discernment? Byagghapajja, a son of good family
discerns. He has the discernment that leads to arising and passing away —
noble, penetrating, rightly leading to the wearing away of dissatisfaction.
This is called accomplishment in discernment.

Byagghapajja, these are the four things that lead to the welfare and happiness
in the life to come of a son of good family.

### Verses

Getting up to do his work,
heedful and capable,
he lives in balance
and guards what he has gathered.

With confidence, accomplished in conduct,
openhanded, free of stinginess,
he keeps clearing the path
to well-being in the life to come.

These eight things
of a householder with confidence
were proclaimed by the one truly named
as bringing happiness in both worlds:

welfare in this life
and happiness in the life to come.
This is how, for householders,
generosity and benefit grow."

The fourth discourse is finished.
