# AN 11.12 Translation Notes

This document records the source and editorial decisions for
[AN 11.12: Dutiyamahānāma Sutta](an11-12-dutiyamahanama-sutta.md).

## Source Base

- Primary source: SuttaCentral Bilara Mahāsaṅgīti root text, segment IDs
  `an11.12:1.1`–`an11.12:5.17`.
- Canonical reader source: <https://suttacentral.net/an11.12/pli/ms>.
- Expansion control: the parallel first discourse with Mahānāma,
  <https://suttacentral.net/an11.11/pli/ms>, together with the recollection
  formulas already translated in MN 7.
- The discourse has about 367 Pali words after front matter is excluded.

## Governing Decisions

- **Remembering remains an action.** The six compounds are rendered
  `remembering the Buddha`, `remembering the Dhamma`, `remembering the
  Saṅgha`, `remembering your own conduct`, `remembering your own generosity`,
  and `remembering the devas`. The section title `Six Things to Remember`
  makes the practical task immediately visible without using `mindfulness`.
- **`citta` is `heart`.** Here the term names the affective or feeling mind:
  it may be taken over by passion, aversion, or confusion; pointed straight;
  and brought into composure. `Heart` keeps this distinct from `mano`, the
  thinking mind, which does not occur in this discourse.
- **`samādhi` is `composure`.** The closing sequence describes the affective
  and cognitive life coming together rather than fighting. The translation
  therefore says that the heart `becomes composed`, not concentrated.
- **The positive sequence keeps four governed states distinct.** `Pāmojja` is
  an `actively happy state` (with `active gladness` used once as a lighter
  back-reference), `pīti` is `rejoicing`, `passaddhi` is `relaxation`, and
  `sukha` is `satisfaction`. This lets a newcomer follow the movement from
  deliberate gladdening through bodily relaxation into composure.
- **`ujugata citta` is a heart `pointed straight`.** This is more concrete in
  English than `upright mind` and fits the passage's repeated statement that
  each recollection gives the heart a wholesome direction.
- **`rāga`, `dosa`, and `moha` are `passion`, `aversion`, and `confusion`.**
  `Pariyuṭṭhita` is `taken over`, preserving its active sense without
  turning the line into abstract diagnostic language.
- **The setting stays ordinary.** `Puttasambādhasayana` is rendered `living at
  home with your children around you`. The point is that the practice works
  amid family pressure as well as in formal postures or at work.

## Peyyāla Handling

AN 11.12 prints the Buddha recollection and its result, then abbreviates the
Dhamma, Saṅgha, conduct, and generosity recollections with `…pe…`. It prints
the deva recollection and the same result again. The reader edition expands
the four middle recollections from the parallel AN 11.11 formulas and the
repository's established recollection language.

The Pali's shared result—freedom from takeover, straightening of the heart,
inspiration, active gladness, rejoicing, relaxation, satisfaction, and
composure—would occur after each recollection in the expanded text. Repeating
that paragraph six times would obscure the practical pattern for a new
reader, so the edition lists all six recollections first and states their
shared result once. No new stage or result has been added.

The Buddha and Saṅgha formulas themselves contain internal abbreviations in
the supplied root. They are restored from their standard full forms. The deva
realms are also expanded through the usual sequence represented by the
source's abbreviation.

## Source-Audit Corrections

- AN 11.12 directly supports six recollection compounds: `buddhānussati`,
  `dhammānussati`, `saṅghānussati`, `sīlānussati`, `cāgānussati`, and
  `devatānussati`.
- It does **not** contain `upasamānussati`, recollection of peace. That false
  glossary citation is replaced with the exact list source at AN 1.296-305.
- The same audit found that SN 55.30 contains `ariyasāvaka`, noble disciple,
  and an abbreviated Saṅgha formula, but not the compound `ariyapuggala`.
  Its direct citation is removed from that term record rather than using the
  discourse as a misleading translation priority.

## Wave 10 Role

AN 11.12 opens Wave 10. It was chosen after direct source inspection rejected
SN 55.30 as a false major-term leverage signal. The discourse anchors six
verified recollection terms in a compact practice that can be used while
moving, working, or living with family.
