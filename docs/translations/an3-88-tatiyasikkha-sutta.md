# AN 3.88: Tatiyasikkhā Sutta (The Third Discourse on Training)

## Editorial Note

- Companion translator notes:
  [AN 3.88 Translation Notes](an3-88-tatiyasikkha-sutta-notes.md)
- Primary source: the Mahāsaṅgīti Pali text in SuttaCentral's Bilara data.
- More than 150 monastic rules are gathered into three trainings: higher
  conduct, higher mind, and higher discernment.
- The traditional attainment ladder is translated as a series of plain
  outcomes. The Pali category names and the handling of repeated formulas are
  documented in the companion notes.
- The closing point is kept direct: complete training brings complete success,
  partial training brings partial success, and none of the training is wasted.

## Translation

### Three Trainings Cover Everything

"Bhikkhus, every two weeks more than 150 training rules are recited. Young men
from respectable families who want what is good for themselves train in them.

All of these rules are included in three trainings. Which three?

- training in higher conduct
- training in higher mind
- training in higher discernment

These are the three trainings that include them all.

### Firm in What Matters

Here a bhikkhu completely fulfills conduct, composure, and discernment.

They may break some lesser and minor training rules, then make things right and
return to good standing.

Why is that? I do not say that a minor offense makes them incapable of
success.

But in the training rules that are fundamental and fitting to the spiritual
life, their conduct is constant and steady. They undertake those rules and
train in them.

### How Far the Training Can Lead

With the wearing away of the outflows, they realize for themselves release of
mind and release through discernment, both free of outflows. They enter and
live in these releases here and now.

If they do not get that far, then with the complete wearing away of the five
lower fetters, they may reach final nibbāna in one of five ways:

- between one life and the next
- soon after being reborn
- without extra effort
- with extra effort
- by heading upstream to Akaniṭṭha, the Highest Realm

If they do not get that far, then with the complete wearing away of three
fetters and the thinning of passion, aversion, and confusion, they are a
once-returner. They return to this world just one more time and then make an
end of dissatisfaction.

If they do not get that far, then with the complete wearing away of three
fetters, they may:

- live one more human life and then make an end of dissatisfaction
- be reborn in two or three families and then make an end of dissatisfaction
- be reborn at most seven times among gods and humans and then make an end of
  dissatisfaction

### None of the Training Is Wasted

So someone who trains fully succeeds fully. Someone who trains partly succeeds
partly.

I tell you: none of these training rules is wasted."

The eighth discourse is finished.
