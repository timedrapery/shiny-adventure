## Translation

### The Setting

At one time the Buddha was staying at Nātika, in the brick house. There the
Buddha addressed the bhikkhus: "Bhikkhus."

"Bhadante," the bhikkhus replied. The Buddha said this:

"Bhikkhus, when recollection of death is developed and cultivated, it bears
great fruit and great benefit. It plunges into the deathless and ends in the
deathless. Do you develop recollection of death?"

### Six Bhikkhus Answer

When this was said, one bhikkhu said to the Buddha: "Bhante, I develop
recollection of death."

"And how do you develop it, bhikkhu?"

"Bhante, I think: 'If only I might live for a night and a day, I would attend
to the Buddha's instruction. I would have done a great deal.' That is how I
develop recollection of death."

Another bhikkhu said to the Buddha: "Bhante, I too develop recollection of
death."

"And how do you develop it, bhikkhu?"

"Bhante, I think: 'If only I might live for a day, I would attend to the
Buddha's instruction. I would have done a great deal.' That is how I develop
recollection of death."

Another bhikkhu said to the Buddha: "Bhante, I too develop recollection of
death."

"And how do you develop it, bhikkhu?"

"Bhante, I think: 'If only I might live for as long as it takes to eat one meal
of alms food, I would attend to the Buddha's instruction. I would have done a
great deal.' That is how I develop recollection of death."

Another bhikkhu said to the Buddha: "Bhante, I too develop recollection of
death."

"And how do you develop it, bhikkhu?"

"Bhante, I think: 'If only I might live for as long as it takes to chew and
swallow four or five mouthfuls, I would attend to the Buddha's instruction. I
would have done a great deal.' That is how I develop recollection of death."

Another bhikkhu said to the Buddha: "Bhante, I too develop recollection of
death."

"And how do you develop it, bhikkhu?"

"Bhante, I think: 'If only I might live for as long as it takes to chew and
swallow one mouthful, I would attend to the Buddha's instruction. I would have
done a great deal.' That is how I develop recollection of death."

Another bhikkhu said to the Buddha: "Bhante, I too develop recollection of
death."

"And how do you develop it, bhikkhu?"

"Bhante, I think: 'If only I might live for as long as it takes to breathe in
after breathing out, or to breathe out after breathing in, I would attend to
the Buddha's instruction. I would have done a great deal.' That is how I
develop recollection of death."

### Heedless and Heedful

When this was said, the Buddha said to the bhikkhus:

"Bhikkhus, there is the bhikkhu who develops recollection of death like this:
'If only I might live for a night and a day, I would attend to the Buddha's
instruction. I would have done a great deal.'

There is the bhikkhu who develops recollection of death like this: 'If only I
might live for a day, I would attend to the Buddha's instruction. I would have
done a great deal.'

There is the bhikkhu who develops recollection of death like this: 'If only I
might live for as long as it takes to eat one meal of alms food, I would attend
to the Buddha's instruction. I would have done a great deal.'

There is the bhikkhu who develops recollection of death like this: 'If only I
might live for as long as it takes to chew and swallow four or five mouthfuls,
I would attend to the Buddha's instruction. I would have done a great deal.'

These bhikkhus, it is said, live heedlessly. They develop recollection of death
slowly for the wearing away of the outflows.

But there is the bhikkhu who develops recollection of death like this: 'If only
I might live for as long as it takes to chew and swallow one mouthful, I would
attend to the Buddha's instruction. I would have done a great deal.'

There is the bhikkhu who develops recollection of death like this: 'If only I
might live for as long as it takes to breathe in after breathing out, or to
breathe out after breathing in, I would attend to the Buddha's instruction. I
would have done a great deal.'

These bhikkhus, it is said, live heedfully. They develop recollection of death
keenly for the wearing away of the outflows.

So, bhikkhus, you should train like this: 'We will live heedfully. We will
develop recollection of death keenly for the wearing away of the outflows.'
That is how you should train."

The ninth discourse is finished.
