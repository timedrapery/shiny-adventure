# AN 2.9: Cariya Sutta

## Editorial Note

- Companion translator notes:
  [AN 2.9 Translation Notes](an2-9-cariya-sutta-notes.md)
- Primary source: the Mahāsaṅgīti Pali text in SuttaCentral's Bilara data.
- `hiri` and `ottappa` are rendered as `conscience` and `moral caution`.
- The ancient social example concerns sexual boundaries within families and
  relationships. It has been kept direct rather than hidden behind vague
  language about "social order."

## Translation

### Two Bright Qualities

"Bhikkhus, two bright qualities protect the world.

Which two?

Conscience and moral caution.

If these two bright qualities did not protect the world, people would stop
respecting even the most basic sexual boundaries: mother, mother's sister,
uncle's wife, teacher's wife, or the wife of someone they look up to.

The world would lose all sexual boundaries, like goats and sheep, chickens and
pigs, dogs and jackals.

But because these two bright qualities do protect the world, people respect
the boundaries around a mother, a mother's sister, an uncle's wife, a teacher's
wife, and the wife of someone they look up to."

The ninth discourse is finished.
