A deity asks one direct question: how did the Buddha cross a flood? His answer
sounds impossible. Standing still makes him sink, while struggling makes him
get swept away. He crosses by doing neither.

The text does not define the flood here. The image is enough to begin with:
it is something powerful that can overwhelm you and carry you along. The
answer also does not mean "make no effort." Standing still and struggling are
both shown to fail. The short exchange asks whether there is a way of moving
that is neither passive collapse nor frantic forcing.

Read the dialogue once before opening the word explanations. Its unresolved
tension is the teaching, not a puzzle the introduction should solve for you.
