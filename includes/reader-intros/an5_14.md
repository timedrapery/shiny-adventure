The *five strengths* come up again and again in the texts, usually as a bare
list. This short discourse says what each one is.

They are the same five qualities as the five faculties in SN 48.10, and they
are defined in nearly the same words: confidence, energy, remembering,
unification, and discernment. The traditional explanation is that the
faculties are the capacities, and the strengths are those capacities once
they hold, so that their opposites cannot shake them.

Each definition is practical. Confidence is confidence in the Buddha's
awakening. Energy is not putting down the burden. Remembering is calling to
mind what was done and said long ago. Unification is the four mental themes.
Discernment is the kind of understanding that sees things arise and pass
away, and leads to the end of dissatisfaction.
