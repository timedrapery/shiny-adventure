The *observance day* is the day each week, by the moon's phases, when lay
followers traditionally take on extra precepts for a day and a night. This
short discourse lists the eight that make up the observance.

The method is simple and repeated eight times. The disciple reflects that
the arahants, the fully awakened, live in a certain way for their whole
lives, and says: today, for this night and day, I will live that way too.

The first four match the precepts most people know: not killing, not
stealing, not lying, and here, for the day, complete celibacy. The fifth is
not taking intoxicants. The last three are what make it a day apart: eating
once, giving up shows and adornment, and sleeping on a plain, low bed.
