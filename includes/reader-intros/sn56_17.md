In everyday speech, ignorance can mean simply not having enough facts. Here it
has a narrower, practical meaning: not seeing dissatisfaction, where it comes
from, when it ends, and how to end it again.

The Pali source abbreviates the final four lines and leaves the reciter to
supply the pattern. This edition prints all four. The point is not just to know
a definition of ignorance, but to apply yourself to seeing each part of the
process.
