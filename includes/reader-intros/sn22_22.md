This short text asks you to picture your whole experience as a load you are
carrying.

The load is the five *clung-to heaps*: form, felt experience, recognition,
putting-together activities and knowing, when they are taken personally. The
one carrying it is simply the person, called here by name and clan. Picking
the load up is *ignorant wanting*, and putting it down is that wanting fading
and ending.

If you have read the four noble truths, you will recognize the last two
answers: they are the same words, about the origin and the quenching of
dissatisfaction. The image says what those truths mean in practice. The
weight is not life itself; it is the taking up.

The closing verses add one more thing. Putting the burden down does not mean
picking up a different one, and the wanting is pulled out by the root, not
just set aside for a while.
