This chapter of the Dhammapada is about the Buddha: what it means that he
appeared, and what he taught.

It contains the verse many people treat as the whole teaching in four lines:
*not doing any evil, taking up the wholesome, purifying your own mind: this
is the teaching of the Buddhas.* The verses around it fill in the daily
shape of that: patience, not harming anyone, moderation in eating, a quiet
place to sit, and devotion to the higher mind.

Later verses ask what a real refuge is. Frightened people take refuge in
mountains, forests, and shrines. The refuge that works is the Buddha, the
Dhamma, and the Saṅgha, and the text defines it by what you see: the four
noble truths.

The numbers beside the verses are the Dhammapada's own verse numbers.
