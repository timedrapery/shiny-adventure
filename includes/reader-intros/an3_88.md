This begins with an intimidating number: more than 150 monastic rules, recited
every two weeks. The Buddha gathers all of them into three things to train:
conduct, the mind, and discernment.

The long middle section lists traditional degrees of completion. You do not
need to memorize them. Its point is the closing line: complete training brings
complete success, partial training still brings partial success, and none of
the work is wasted. Small mistakes can be repaired, while the commitments
fundamental to the spiritual life must remain steady.
