This is one of the shortest texts in the collection, and one of the most
quoted.

It makes three claims. Everything that has been *put together* is
impermanent. Everything that has been put together is unsatisfactory. And
all phenomena are not-self.

The frame around each claim matters as much as the claim. These things hold
whether or not a Buddha ever appears. A Buddha does not make them true. He
awakens to them, and then he makes them plain to others.

If the wording of the frame sounds familiar, it is the same wording SN 12.20
uses for dependent arising.
