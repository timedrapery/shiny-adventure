This short text calls conscience and moral caution "bright" because they stop
a person before they do harm. Conscience is the inward sense that an action
would betray your own integrity. Moral caution looks outward, at the harm an
action would cause.

The example comes from an ancient setting and is about sexual boundaries in
families and relationships. Its wider point is simple: a human world works only
when people hold themselves back, even when no one makes them.
